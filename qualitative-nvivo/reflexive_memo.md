# Reflexive memo

My developing Naturalistic Behavioural Therapy ideas emphasise environmental contingencies and behaviour. This could predispose me to notice task ambiguity, immediate reinforcement and behavioural initiation while underweighting identity, interpersonal expectations or cultural explanations.

To manage that risk, I would:
- record my theoretical assumptions before and during analysis;
- actively search for disconfirming or deviant cases;
- distinguish descriptive coding from interpretive theme development;
- avoid treating frequency as equivalent to importance;
- document changes to candidate themes;
- avoid presenting themes as objectively "discovered" entities independent of the researcher.

This memo is part of the audit trail and demonstrates reflexive awareness rather than claiming neutrality.
