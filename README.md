# Psychology Research Methods Portfolio

Practical competency demonstrations supporting Elliot Ledson's Psychology BSc Recognition of Prior Learning portfolio.

## Scope

This repository contains three deliberately bounded demonstrations:

1. **quantitative-spss/** - a synthetic psychology-style dataset analysed reproducibly in Python, with equivalent SPSS syntax.
2. **qualitative-nvivo/** - fictional qualitative material, a manual codebook and coded-excerpt audit workflow suitable for NVivo-style organisation.
3. **research-ethics/** - a fictional research protocol, structured risk register and a protocol-completeness checker.

## Important provenance statement

No real participants were recruited and no human or animal research was conducted for these demonstrations. Data and transcript material are synthetic or fictional. The repository demonstrates analytical, methodological and documentation skills. It does not claim ethics approval, completed empirical research, formal SPSS/NVivo certification or awarded academic credit.

The purpose is to make prior and transferable competence inspectable and reproducible, while leaving Psychology-specific competence open to formal academic assessment.
