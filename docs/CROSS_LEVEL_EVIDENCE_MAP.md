# Cross-Level Psychology Evidence Map

This repository supports Elliot Ledson's Psychology BSc Recognition of Prior Learning portfolio. It should be read alongside the Google Drive Level 4, Level 5 and Level 6 folders.

## Level 4

Primary contribution:
- quantitative analysis workflow and SPSS syntax
- qualitative coding workflow suitable for NVivo organisation
- ethics reasoning and research documentation

Use:
These artefacts strengthen research-methods evidence for Beginning as a Researcher and Developing as a Researcher. They are current practical demonstrations, not historical assessed submissions.

## Level 5

Primary contribution:
- reproducible quantitative workflow
- qualitative coding and audit trail
- research ethics documentation
- explicit distinction between prediction and psychological inference

Use:
These artefacts support Psychological Research Training and Psychological Research in Action by demonstrating practical method competence beyond essay-only evidence.

Boundary:
No participant study has been conducted in this repository. Synthetic and fictional materials must not be presented as empirical participant research.

## Level 6

Primary contribution:
- methods readiness for a 40-credit empirical psychology project
- reproducibility
- analysis planning
- ethics and safeguarding documentation
- transparent provenance

Use:
The repository supports the research-methods infrastructure needed for the Applied Psychology Project, but it does not substitute for ethics approval, participant recruitment, laboratory/field data collection, supervision or a completed dissertation.

## Programme-lead checklist

The portfolio is structured to address competencies explicitly raised in correspondence:
- SPSS
- NVivo
- thematic coding
- data analysis
- research ethics
- vulnerable-participant ethics
- animal-research ethics
- related core psychological research competencies

Each competency should be assessed on its own evidence rather than inferred from software-engineering experience.
