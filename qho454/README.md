# QHO454 — Beginning as a Researcher

This folder maps the existing psychology research-methods portfolio to the released QHO454 Portfolio 1 and Portfolio 2 materials and to the concrete marking behaviours rewarded in the issued briefs, task instructions and formative feedback.

## Released assessment structure

### Portfolio 1 — 50% of module

1. **Methods section critique** — 500 words
2. **Describing and displaying data** — 500 words

Submitted as one document. No A1 cover page or contents page is required.

### Portfolio 2 — 50% of module

3. **Ethics application completion** — 500 words
4. **Interview schedule critique** — 500 words

Submitted as one document using the supplied A2 cover page and contents structure.

## Learning outcomes

The released briefs identify five learning outcomes:

- **LO1** — Explain the use of varying quantitative and qualitative approaches to the study of Psychology.
- **LO2** — Identify and apply a variety of quantitative and qualitative research methods and analytical techniques in differing real-world psychological contexts.
- **LO3** — Consider the ethical implications of different study designs and conduct research under close supervision.
- **LO4** — Identify and reflect on values central to practice in Psychology and respond in an ethical manner in a variety of contexts.
- **LO5** — Apply findings from quantitative and qualitative psychological research to personal, social, community and organisational issues.

## Marking-criteria target

The directory is organised around criterion-level behaviours rather than broad topic coverage.

### Task 1

- identify all major method deficiencies;
- evaluate why they matter;
- propose specific, accurate and convincing corrections;
- organise the critique clearly and succinctly.

### Task 2

- report informative, appropriate, relevant and accurate descriptive statistics;
- demonstrate insight into the data;
- use appropriate reporting conventions;
- present accurate, clearly labelled and cleanly formatted displays.

### Task 3

- demonstrate applied understanding of ethical principles;
- explain how those principles change study design and researcher conduct;
- address data management, consent, withdrawal, confidentiality, participant risk, researcher risk and institutional risk;
- use the supplied ethics application template and complete only the required highlighted fields.

### Task 4

- demonstrate excellent understanding of qualitative interviewing;
- evaluate the schedule's order, content and wording;
- identify both strengths and weaknesses;
- propose convincing alternative questions;
- justify recommendations with appropriate methodological literature.
