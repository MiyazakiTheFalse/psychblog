# Participant Information and Consent Pack

> Simulated portfolio document. No real participant recruitment is taking place.

## Participant Information

### What is the study about?
This study explores whether social isolation and the regularity of everyday routines are associated with psychological wellbeing.

### Why have I been invited?
You may be eligible because you are aged 18 or over and receive community mental-health support.

### Do I have to take part?
No. Taking part is entirely voluntary. Choosing not to participate will not affect your care, services, benefits or relationship with any professional.

### What will I do?
You will complete a short questionnaire about social contact, daily routines and wellbeing. You may skip any question.

### Could anything be uncomfortable?
Some questions may make you think about loneliness, stress or wellbeing. You can pause or stop immediately.

### Can I withdraw?
Yes. You may withdraw without giving a reason. Identifiable data can be deleted until the point described in the study's data-management plan when responses are irreversibly anonymised.

### Is what I say confidential?
Research data will be handled confidentially and stored securely. Confidentiality may be limited if information indicates an immediate and serious risk of harm and safeguarding action is required. This limit must be explained before consent.
