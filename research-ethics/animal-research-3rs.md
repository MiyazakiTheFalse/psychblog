# Animal research ethics: 3Rs note

This portfolio contains no animal research.

My current understanding of the core 3Rs framework is:

- **Replacement**: use non-animal alternatives where they can answer the scientific question adequately.
- **Reduction**: use the minimum number of animals compatible with a scientifically valid design.
- **Refinement**: modify procedures, housing, handling and endpoints to minimise pain, suffering and distress and improve welfare.

Competence in animal research ethics would still require programme-specific teaching on the applicable legal, institutional and professional framework. This note is evidence of conceptual familiarity, not authorisation to conduct animal research.
